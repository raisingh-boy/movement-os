# Movement Intelligence Platform Overview

## Mission
To create an AI-native system capable of learning movement from thousands of videos, descriptions, coaching cues, biomechanics resources, and practitioner experiences. The goal is to build a continuously growing knowledge system about movement, moving beyond simple classification to a deep, multi-dimensional understanding of human motion.

## Vision
Imagine a world where the collective wisdom of thousands of years of human movement—from dance and parkour to gymnastics and somatic practices—is accessible, searchable, and interconnected. The Movement Intelligence Platform aims to be the definitive repository for this knowledge, serving as a bridge between physical practice and digital intelligence.

## Core Pillars
1.  **Continuous Learning**: An AI-driven pipeline that ingests data from diverse sources and refines its understanding over time.
2.  **Multi-Layer Representation**: Moving beyond skeletal data to include intention, sensation, and coaching cues.
3.  **Knowledge Interconnectivity**: A rich graph connecting movements through families, progressions, and prerequisites.
4.  **Expert-in-the-Loop**: A system that values and integrates the embodied knowledge of expert teachers.
5.  **Inclusive and Diverse**: Normalizing across body types and styles without losing individual nuance.
6.  **Scalability**: An architecture designed to handle hundreds of thousands of videos and millions of movement segments.
