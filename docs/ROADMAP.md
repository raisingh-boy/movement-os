# Implementation Roadmap

This roadmap outlines the path from a movement recognition model to a full-scale Movement Intelligence Platform.

## Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1-6)
*   **Pipeline Alpha:** Basic video ingestion from YouTube/Instagram.
*   **DMO Definition:** Finalizing the schema for Deep Movement Objects.
*   **Core Graph:** Seeding the Knowledge Graph with 500 foundational movements from Gymnastics and Parkour.
*   **Basic Web Explorer:** 2D video playback with skeletal overlays.

## Phase 2: Multi-Layer Expansion (Months 7-12)
*   **Somatic Integration:** Launching the expert contribution tool for somatic and attention cues.
*   **NLP Engine:** Automating the extraction of cues from video transcripts.
*   **Diversity Normalization:** Implementing anthropometric scaling for skeletal comparisons.
*   **3D Visualizer Beta:** Introducing the 3D playback environment.

## Phase 3: Intelligence & Scale (Months 13-24)
*   **Automated Discovery:** Deploying unsupervised clustering to find new variations.
*   **Cross-Disciplinary Linker:** Mapping similarities between disparate movement cultures (e.g., Yoga and BJJ).
*   **Scale-Up:** Expanding ingestion to reach 50,000 videos.
*   **API Launch:** Allowing third-party developers to build apps on top of the MKG.

## Phase 4: The Global Knowledge System (Year 3+)
*   **Full Integration:** 100,000+ videos and 1,000+ expert contributors.
*   **Real-time Feedback:** (Optional hardware integration) Providing real-time coaching based on the MKG.
*   **Autonomous Research:** The system generates new hypotheses about movement efficiency and health.
