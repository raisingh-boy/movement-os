# Learning Loop and Expert Contribution

## 1. AI Learning Loop (Question 6)

The system is designed for continuous evolution through an iterative feedback loop.

### Discovering New Knowledge
*   **Novelty Detection:** The system flags video segments that don't map closely to existing DMOs or Knowledge Graph nodes.
*   **Unsupervised Clustering:** Periodically re-clustering the entire segment database to identify emerging variations or styles.
*   **Pattern Merging:** Identifying when two different names/nodes actually describe the same biomechanical and intentional pattern, then merging them with "aliases."

### Knowledge Updating
*   **Confidence Scoring:** Each node and edge has a confidence score based on the volume and quality of supporting data.
*   **Conflict Resolution:** If two sources (e.g., two different coaching videos) provide conflicting cues, the system maintains both as "Context-Specific Cues" rather than forcing a single truth.

## 2. Seamless Expert Layer (Question 7)

Experts are not just data sources; they are active architects of the knowledge system.

### Contribution Interfaces
*   **Annotation Tooling:** A specialized interface where teachers can overlay somatic descriptions, highlight attentional focus areas on a video, and link movements to principles (e.g., "Yield and Push").
*   **Concept Tagging:** Allowing experts to define new "Strategies" or "Sensations" in the Knowledge Graph that AI might not detect automatically.
*   **Peer Review:** Expert contributions are vetted by other recognized teachers in the same discipline before being fully integrated into the "Gold Standard" nodes.

### Valuing Experience
*   **Somatic Anchoring:** The system prioritizes human-described sensations over raw biomechanical data when calculating the "essence" of a movement.
*   **Contextual Weighting:** Cues from a renowned gymnastics coach are weighted more heavily for "Standard Form," while cues from a contemporary dancer are weighted for "Creative Variation."
